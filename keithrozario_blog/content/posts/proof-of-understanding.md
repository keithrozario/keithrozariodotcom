How do you prove to someone that you truly know something?

With AI all around us, it's very easy to pretend or give the impression of knowing, without actually ... knowing.

We've always tested knowledge by asking questions, either through a series of questions in a written exam like school and universities, or through a questions in an oral exam like a job interview. The concept is simple, you claim you understand something, now I'll ask you some questions to see if you truly know the answer.

But in the world of AI, providing answers is easy. So easy that universities and job interviews are going through a difficult phase having to deal with cheaters.
