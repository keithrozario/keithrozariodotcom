I strongly believe the secret to my happiness is two things:

  * Not caring about what other people think
  * Low expectations

I think if you can successfully accomplish these two things, you'll be very happy. But while the secret is simple, it isn't easy. In fact it's hard to not care about what other people, and it's equally hard to have low expectations especially of yourself.
